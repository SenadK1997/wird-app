Wird

Daily dhikr & tasbih

Wird is a simple counter for dhikr and tasbih. It includes the tasbih after prayer, the morning and evening adhkar, collections for protection, provision, forgiveness and hardship, your own dhikr and flows, a daily goal, reminders and a plain free counter.

Everything you count stays on your phone. There is no account, and the app works without an internet connection.
